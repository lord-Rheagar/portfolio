An interactive view of how AI could reshape approximately **142 Indian occupations**. Each rectangle represents an occupation: its size reflects the estimated number of workers, while color can show AI exposure, job outlook, median pay, or education.

The app uses India's NCO 2015 occupation classification and estimates drawn from PLFS 2023–24 and PayScale India. A two-pass LLM process generates labor-market data and scores exposure. **Exposure means potential for disruption, not a prediction of job loss**; many roles may be augmented rather than replaced.

[Explore the live visualization](https://indian-job-ai--bodhiswattwac.replit.app/) and expand its Methodology section for scoring details and caveats.
