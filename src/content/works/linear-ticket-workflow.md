A support workflow that turns relevant Slack messages into structured Linear tickets. A support-team reaction triggers the flow; AI rewrites the issue into a title and body, adds context, assigns a priority, and creates the ticket in Linear.

The [workflow document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1szcKWmPRbx7GONRPOOQuy4tCnJkvF7luUnFCokxCCNI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.tv3ijhmq2qxy) describes the trigger, classification, and testing steps.
