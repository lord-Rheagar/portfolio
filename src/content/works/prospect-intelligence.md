A prospect qualification workflow for WarpBuild. Starting with a company and a public GitHub repository, n8n checks recent GitHub Actions activity and searches engineering pages and job posts for relevant signals. It stores source links in Notion, drafts a reason the company may fit, and applies a 25-point qualification score.

The initial pilot researched **10 companies**: three passed the automated qualification gate and seven remained in review. A person still checks the evidence and decides whether an account merits further research; the workflow does not find contacts or send outreach.

The [full project page](https://lively-plum-5f2.notion.site/WarpBuild-Prospect-Intelligence-3e136b5732a381bdbd3ede6968153cf3) includes the workflow, scoring rules, evidence log, and live prospect views.
