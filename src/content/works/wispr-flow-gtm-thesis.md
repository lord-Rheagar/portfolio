A GTM thesis for Wispr Flow's India launch, built around a creator pipeline of **100+ scored and tiered Indian creators**.

The operational plan contains **16 localized content hooks**, **three outreach templates**, and an AI workflow designed to process each creator in under five minutes. It maps one creator video into seven platform assets and uses an experiment tracker to identify approaches worth repeating.

The [full thesis](https://lively-plum-5f2.notion.site/GTM-Thesis-Wispr-Flow-19236b5732a3809abb91e13e5e3a2522?pvs=74) also covers the market argument, launch channels, creator tiers, and a deployment timeline.
