A research proposal asking whether an AI agent's progress toward an unfinished task changes how often it engages in task gaming.

The proposed method saves low- and high-progress checkpoints from the same task attempt, resumes the agent from each, and compares subsequent behavior. The [full study](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pOiulOahzPe1X3NXZE7kWb-EtxBY7WFmRVpJangxhmU/edit?tab=t.0) discusses the research gap and feasibility of the checkpoint approach.
