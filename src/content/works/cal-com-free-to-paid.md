A conversion plan focused on the moment a happy individual user starts scheduling like a team. It proposes detecting that behavior, introducing team features through timely lifecycle messages, and helping newly paid teams reach their first routed booking.

The plan also calls for checking whether flat revenue is driven more by conversion or by churn, fixing a Round Robin setup failure, and testing a card-free reverse trial. Its **$0.4M–$1.4M first-year incremental ARR range is a modeled opportunity, not a measured result**. A holdout experiment would test whether the conversion messages create real lift.
