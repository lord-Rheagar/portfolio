An n8n workflow that gathers current competitor information with Perplexity and Tavily, uses OpenAI to turn the findings into a structured report, and connects the output to a Google Sheets dashboard.

The [full project document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fmvnCdzqrK0bfb9BihTsRLd9C_UPWVFPEwTRlPEGQ_U/edit?tab=t.0) includes the dashboard, a workflow demo, setup steps, and sample competitor reports.
