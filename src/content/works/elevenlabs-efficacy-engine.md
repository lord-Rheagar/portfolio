An efficacy report that scored ElevenLabs creators by their estimated contribution to the B2B pipeline for ElevenAgents. The analysis found that many of the strongest-fit creators were unpaid while budget was directed toward a less effective tier. Pipeline and cost figures are modeled from public data, rather than measured internal results.

Explore the [interactive dashboard](https://hyperagent.com/s/Zx2t06AA_gLKRx_hhRST7A) and the [underlying model analysis](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1aKRBdzHlwUXwUF2qvRUSatbUO7_tYBigEjjc65mDinc/edit?usp=sharing).
