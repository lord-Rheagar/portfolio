An AI-powered Slack assistant that helps teams find answers in internal SOPs, onboarding guides, and playbooks without searching through long documents.

The n8n workflow watches a Google Drive folder, indexes document chunks with OpenAI embeddings and Pinecone, retrieves relevant passages for a Slack question, and posts an answer back to the channel. The [project document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1szcKWmPRbx7GONRPOOQuy4tCnJkvF7luUnFCokxCCNI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.qavr92m6dkrk) explains the setup and use case.
